Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

Clean Boost is a Mac app that scans and cleans files on your computer. It is built so that your files and what's on them stay on your Mac. This policy explains what the app stores, what it can send, and the choices you have.

What stays on your Mac

  • Scan results, cleaning history (paths and sizes of removed items, kept for 90 days so you can restore them) and your settings are stored locally on your Mac.
  • Clean Boost reads file names, sizes and dates to decide what can be cleaned. It does not upload file names, paths or file contents.
  • Folder access you grant in the Mac App Store version is stored on your Mac and can be changed at any time in Settings.

What may be sent, and when

  • Cleaning rule updates. The app may download updated cleaning rules from our server. This request contains no personal data, but like any internet request it reveals your IP address to the server.
  • Anonymous statistics, off by default. Only if you turn this on in Settings, the app sends aggregate numbers such as how much space was freed and which features were used. It never includes file names, paths or anything that identifies you.
  • Problem reports, only when you send them. Report a Problem builds a report on your Mac. You can read it first, and nothing is sent until you choose to share it.
  • Updates. The Mac App Store version is updated by the App Store. The direct-download version checks our server for new versions.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no third-party tracking or analytics SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No account is required to use the app.

This website

clean.mashtool.com is a static website. It does not use cookies or analytics. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Children

Clean Boost does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions about privacy can be sent through our support page.
