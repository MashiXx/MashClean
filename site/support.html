How can we help?

Answers to common questions about Clean Boost for Mac. Still stuck? Contact us at the bottom of this page.

I deleted something by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes, in most cases. Files you created, and most caches, go to the Trash instead of being deleted. Open History in the sidebar, find the item and click Restore. History keeps records for 90 days, as long as you haven't emptied the Trash.

Why does the Mac App Store version ask for my Home and Applications folders?

Apps from the Mac App Store run in Apple's sandbox and can only read folders you choose. Clean Boost asks for your Home folder to scan caches, logs, large files and duplicates, and for Applications to uninstall apps. Select exactly the folder suggested in the dialog, then click Grant Access. You can change this later in Settings → Permissions.

What is Full Disk Access, and do I need it?

The direct-download version can read data from other apps, such as Mail and Safari caches, when you turn on Full Disk Access in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access. It's optional: without it, Clean Boost scans what it can and labels the categories it couldn't read.

Why are some items not selected after a scan?

Only items marked Safe are selected for you. Items marked Review (like old installers) or Risky (like iPhone backups) may still matter to you, so you decide.

An app won't uninstall.

Quit the app first; in the Mac App Store version Clean Boost waits for you to quit it. Apps installed by the system or owned by another user can't be removed by the Mac App Store version. Apps from the Mac App Store can also be removed from Launchpad.

Where are Login Items and Maintenance in the App Store version?

These tools need permissions that Apple's sandbox doesn't allow. They're available in the direct download from our website.

How do I switch between English and Vietnamese?

Use the language menu at the bottom of the sidebar, or Settings → General. Clean Boost restarts to apply the new language.

How do I remove Clean Boost completely?

Turn off the menu bar item in Settings, then drag Clean Boost from Applications to the Trash. If you installed the admin helper in the direct-download version, remove it first in Settings → Permissions → Remove helper.
